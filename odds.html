How to read this board. Green cells are the best available price for that outcome across every book shown. The "best place to bet" badge goes to the book holding the most best prices for that game. The 📊 consensus line is the median number across every book listed — the reference point sharps use. A ⚡ on a price means that book is a full point (spread/total) or ~3% of win probability (moneyline) off the consensus: a possibly stale line or a deliberate lean — either way, that's where the value, or the trap, lives. ▲▼ marks movement since your last refresh. The ⚖️ Sure bets strip flags cross-book arbitrage — every outcome covered at different books, with the dutch-book stake split for $100; arbs vanish in seconds and books limit arb bettors fast, so confirm both prices before you bet. Tap any price to add it to your slip — the Slip button (bottom-right) combines your legs into parlay odds with a potential payout. Odds update when books move them — always confirm the final price in the sportsbook app before betting.
