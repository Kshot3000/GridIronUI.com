Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026. Short version: we don't run accounts, we don't sell data, and most of your settings never leave your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Preferences — your theme, your Odds API key, your DFS pool, and similar settings are stored in your browser's localStorage. We never receive or see them.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no sign-ups, no passwords.
  • No sale or rental of personal information — we collect essentially none.

Third parties

  • Google AdSense — display ads are served by Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Google ad settings.
  • Google Fonts — fonts load from Google's servers; Google may log the request.
  • YouTube embeds (Watch page) — playing a video loads Google's player, subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Data feeds — when you open live-data pages, your browser fetches public data directly from ESPN, Polymarket, Open-Meteo, and (with your own key) The Odds API. Those requests go to their servers, not ours.
  • Outbound links — clicking a sportsbook or partner link takes you to their site and their policies.

Children

This site is for adults 21+. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.
