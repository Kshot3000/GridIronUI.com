Auto-fill reads the closing price from this browser's saved odds-board snapshots — consensus away-spread / over-total price, or your picked team's consensus moneyline price for moneyline bets (real board data only) — for bets logged on game day. A bet with a manually recorded close is never overwritten — and anything without a matching snapshot stays blank.

Private by design: your journal lives in this browser's localStorage — GridIronUI has no servers, no accounts, nothing is sent anywhere. Export CSV regularly to back it up — the same file imports right back in — and remember that clearing browser data clears the journal. If betting stops being fun, help is free and confidential.
