How affiliate links work here

Some links to sportsbooks and services on GridIronUI are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up or deposit, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That revenue pays for hosting, data feeds, and development.

Our rules

  • Licensed operators only. All outbound links on this site lead exclusively to licensed and authorized gambling operators. We will never link an offshore or unlicensed book, paid or not.
  • Content is independent. Commissions never buy rankings, ratings, or favorable coverage. If a book is bad, we'll say so — or we won't list it.
  • Clearly labeled. Pages or sections containing affiliate links say so where the links appear.
  • Never at your expense. A commission doesn't change the odds, bonuses, or terms you receive — those come from the operator.

What this means for you

Use our links if you find the site useful; don't if you don't. Either way, only bet with licensed operators in your state, and only what you can afford to lose. See legality and responsible gambling.
