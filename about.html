Built for bettors who do the work

GridIronUI is a free sports betting knowledge hub: honest guides, real calculators, live odds and data, and a DFS lab — built to be the site you'd open every morning.

What we believe

  • Math over marketing. Every tool shows its work. No "AI picks", no guaranteed winners, no hype.
  • Honest data. Live feeds say where they come from and when they fail. Samples are labeled as samples.
  • Education first. A bettor who understands implied probability and bankroll management beats a bettor with "locks" every time.
  • Licensed operators only. All outbound links on this site lead exclusively to licensed and authorized gambling operators.

What you'll find here

  • Learn — betting basics, bet types, bankroll management, live betting, reading line movement, the player props playbook, advanced strategy, and a full glossary.
  • Tools — odds converter, implied probability, parlay math, Kelly staking, and a no-vig calculator.
  • Live data — odds board, prediction markets, win probabilities, scores, news, injuries, game-day weather.
  • DFS Lab — a real optimizer for DraftKings and FanDuel, cash and GPP.
  • Watch — betting shows and analysis from major outlets.
