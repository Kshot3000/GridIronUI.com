Game-day weather

Kickoff temperature, wind and precipitation for every upcoming NFL game, mapped to the actual stadium.

Why bettors care: wind moves totals more than rain. Sustained winds above ~15 mph suppress the passing game and field goals — historically pushing games under. Rain matters too, but mostly by pushing teams to run. Dome and retractable-roof games are marked so you don't handicap weather that isn't there.

⚾ MLB postseason weather

First-pitch temperature, wind and precipitation for every upcoming postseason game, mapped to the actual ballpark. This section only appears during the MLB postseason.

Why it matters in October: wind direction moves baseball totals. Wind blowing out boosts home runs; wind in from the outfield suppresses them — we show the direction, you check it against the park. And unlike football, rain can delay or postpone a game — a PPD voids most bets. Retractable-roof parks are marked; check whether the roof is open before first pitch.

Forecasts: Open-Meteo (hourly, matched to kickoff/first pitch) · Venue coordinates: GridIronUI dataset, all 30 NFL stadiums and all 30 MLB ballparks.
