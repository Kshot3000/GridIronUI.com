Prediction markets

Real prices from real traders. A price of 62¢ means the market thinks it happens 62% of the time — no pundits, no hot takes, just money talking. Polymarket streams in live; the Kalshi · NFL, Kalshi · NCAAF and Kalshi · MLB tabs below read snapshots refreshed regularly (Kalshi's API won't talk to browsers directly).

How to read these. Each market is binary — Yes/No, Team A/Team B — and the two prices always sum to ~100¢. When a price disagrees with your own number, that's a potential edge; when it agrees with the sportsbooks, that's confirmation. The 7d badge shows how far the price has moved in the last week (reported by Polymarket), and the book line shows the live bid/ask spread — momentum and liquidity worth knowing before you bet. On the Kalshi tabs, ▲/▼ badges mark prices that moved 2¢ or more since the previous snapshot — the crowd's freshest lean, straight from the order book. The Market pulse strip above the Kalshi cards names the tab's biggest snapshot-to-snapshot moves in one glance; each chip jumps to its game's card. Games involving a team you ★-follow on the odds board are marked ★ Your team on every tab here too, with a jump strip above the cards — a followed Kalshi game hiding behind "Show all" expands the list when its chip is tapped. Looking for one game on a full tab? Find a game above the board filters the active tab's games as you type — on the Kalshi tabs it searches every game in the snapshot, including the ones behind "Show all". Never treat a market price as a prediction about the game — it is the crowd's probability estimate, with real money behind it.

The Kalshi · NFL, Kalshi · NCAAF and Kalshi · MLB (postseason) tabs above show real Kalshi game-winner prices. Kalshi's public API blocks requests from browsers, so instead of faking a live feed, a server-side script pulls their order book regularly and commits timestamped snapshots this page renders — each card says when its prices were captured, and a stale snapshot says so out loud.
