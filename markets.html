Prediction markets

Real prices from real traders. A price of 62¢ means the market thinks it happens 62% of the time — no pundits, no hot takes, just money talking.

How to read these. Each market is binary — Yes/No, Team A/Team B — and the two prices always sum to ~100¢. When a price disagrees with your own number, that's a potential edge; when it agrees with the sportsbooks, that's confirmation. Never treat a market price as a prediction about the game — it is the crowd's probability estimate, with real money behind it.
