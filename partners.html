Prediction-market programs, concretely

We're an independent site — enrolled in neither program below today. But "partner with us" should mean something specific, so here's what's publicly documented as of September 29, 2026, and where GridIronUI would plug in:

  • Polymarket Builder program — their published builder docs define three tiers: Unverified (default — 100 transactions/day, builder API keys from your profile, gasless trading), Verified (manual approval — 1,500 transactions/day, RevShare protocol access, a verified affiliate badge, weekly rewards and grants eligibility), and Partner (a custom split on Polymarket's base fee). The Verified tier is our lane: our markets page and predictions page already consume their public Gamma API with full attribution.
  • Kalshi refer-a-friend — $25 in bonus credits to both sides of a qualified referral (new user, identity verification, a $25 trade), up to $1,000 across 40 referrals; credits expire 7 days after issuance and exact terms vary by account. It rewards real new traders, not clicks — which is how a referral program should work.
