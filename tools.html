Fourteen tools that do the sportsbook math for you — and keep your record honest. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data leaves your device. -110 = 1.91 = 10/11 = 52.38%, every time.

Vig remover / fair odds

Ticket hedge planner

One leg left on a live ticket? Enter your original stake, what the ticket pays in full (stake included), and the price on the other side of the remaining leg — then lock equal profit, free-roll, or size your own plan.

Cash-out evaluator

The book flashes a cash-out number mid-game — is it a fair price, or is the book pocketing the difference? Enter your original bet, the current price on the same side (check the odds board for the best one), and the book's offer. Fair value, the book's cut, and the verdict — plus what hedging the other side yourself would lock in.

Sharp money

Dutching calculator

Backing several outcomes where only one can win — a few golfers, a couple of division winners? Split your stake so the payout is identical whichever one lands. Name each selection (optional) and enter its price.

Sharp money

Teaser calculator

Buy points on every leg of a parlay — the classic NFL 6-pointer. Enter each leg's line and the side you're teasing, and the calculator shows the teased lines, which key numbers each leg crosses, and whether it's a Wong leg (crossing both 3 and 7 — the only teaser construction with real research behind it).

Bankroll risk simulator

Monte Carlo: run thousands of simulated seasons at your win rate and price to see how a bankroll actually behaves — the losing streaks included. This is a model, not a prediction.
