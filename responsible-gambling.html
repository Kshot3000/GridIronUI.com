Play safe. Or don't play.

Betting should be entertainment with a hard budget — like concert tickets, not like rent. If it stops feeling that way, help is free, confidential, and one call away.

24/7

Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER — free, confidential help, any hour. You can also text SUPPORT to 53342 or chat at ncpgambling.org.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned — or chasing losses to "get even".
  • Borrowing money, selling things, or dipping into bills to bet.
  • Lying to people close to you about how much you wager.
  • Betting to escape stress, boredom, anxiety or depression.
  • Needing bigger bets to feel the same rush.
  • Can't stop, cut back, or take a break even when you want to.

If two or more of those sound familiar, treat it seriously — not someday, today.

Rules that keep it fun

  1. A hard bankroll, in dollars, per month — written down before you bet. When it's gone, you're done until next month.
  2. 1–2% stakes. Flat bet sizing removes the emotion from every decision. (See the bankroll guide.)
  3. Never chase. Doubling down after losses is the fastest documented route to ruin.
  4. Never bet drunk, tilted, or desperate. If you're emotional, the apps can wait.
  5. Set the sportsbook's own limits — every legal book offers deposit, wager and time limits. Use them before you need them.
  6. Track every bet. Most losing bettors have no idea they're losing. A spreadsheet doesn't lie.

Tools that help

  • Deposit / loss / time limits — in every legal sportsbook's responsible-gaming settings.
  • Cool-off periods — 24 hours to 30 days locked out, self-serve.
  • State self-exclusion lists — voluntary bans from 1 year to lifetime; search "[your state] self-exclusion".
  • GamBan / blocking software — blocks gambling sites and apps across your devices.
